Deaths by days on feed (DOF)

When in a lot's life are deaths happening? Helps identify whether early-arrival stress, mid-period BRD, or late-stage issues drive losses. Calculated using each death's date minus the lot's arrival date. Anonymous bulk-entered deaths use that event's date as a single point — accurate enough at the cohort level.
