Journal entry request - lot tags on hand at 30 September 2026
Prepared 2 October 2026 for Jayci and Brenda

WHAT HAPPENED

Lot tags were billed to us last month and charged in bulk to cattle, so
the whole purchase is in September expense. 167 of them had not been used
as of the morning of 1 October - they were sitting on Jake Taylor's shelf,
where our processing medicine is held. Those 167 are inventory at 30
September, not September expense.

THE ENTRY

  Date: 30 September 2026

  Debit   Medicine & supplies inventory (asset)          67.74
  Credit  [the expense account the bulk lot-tag bill hit]       67.74

  Memo: Capitalize 167 lot tags on hand at 30 Sep 2026, billed in bulk to
        cattle during September. 167 tags at $0.4056 each (50 for $20.28).

Please map the credit to whichever account took the original bill - we
think it is the same one the cattle processing supplies run through, but
you have the invoice.

HOW IT UNWINDS

  9 tags were used on 1 October, $3.65. That expenses in October, out of
  the asset.

  158 tags remain, $64.08. They stay as inventory until used, and the
  medicine inventory system now draws them down as cattle are processed.

ONE RECONCILING ITEM, SO IT DOES NOT SURPRISE ANYBODY

The medicine inventory system carries these tags from 1 October, not 30
September. The reason is that Jake Taylor's September count is posted and
closed, and the system will not let stock be dated into a closed count -
that lock is what keeps the count and the books from drifting.

So if you tie the inventory report to the general ledger at 30 September,
the ledger will show $67.74 that the report does not. One line, this
entry, and it agrees again from 1 October onward.

If you would rather not have that one-line difference at 30 September,
date the entry 1 October instead. The trade is that September then keeps
the full bulk expense, including the tags nobody used. We think 30
September is the better answer and that is how it is written above, but it
is your call.
