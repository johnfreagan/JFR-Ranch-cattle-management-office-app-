Two things still open

  1. Protivity needs a cost. There are eight 10-dose boxes on the shelf — 80 doses — and Redwing carries none, so this one runs the opposite way to everything above: the books are short, not long. We have left it out of the count rather than valuing it at zero, because a guessed cost would work its way into the cost of every animal treated with it. If you can find what a box cost, it goes into both sides and neither is distorted.
  2. Two bottles of Ivomec Long Range were on the shelf that Redwing has never carried on any line. They are going back to the vendor, so there is nothing to post — but together with the Excede posting and Multi Min's fourth bottle, that is three things in one day that point at receiving rather than at the inventory itself. Worth a look at how deliveries are being booked in.
